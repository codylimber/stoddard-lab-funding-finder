    Programs are compiled from each funder's official page and from Princeton's public Graduate School and department pages. Open means a deadline is posted. Rolling means applications are accepted at any time. Watch means the program recurs but its next deadline is not posted yet. Many Princeton funds are applied for in SAFE (the Student Activity Funding Engine), where the current dates live. Deadlines move: check the linked page before committing.
